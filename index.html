A Legacy in Flight

Twenty Years of Commitment, Capability and Pride.

This anniversary recognises the people, dedication and teamwork behind the Sukhoi Su-30MKM journey in service with the Royal Malaysian Air Force.

As we look ahead, we honour the experience gained and the commitment that continues to guide the squadron into the future.

MISSION

TO CARRY OUT AIR OPERATION EFFECTIVELY AND EFFICIENTLY BY USING ALL AVAILABLE RESOURCES TO MEET THE RMAF OPERATIONAL READINESS REQUIREMENT AT ALL TIMES.

VISION

ACHIEVE AND MAINTAIN THE LEVEL OF CAPABILITY REQUIRED TO BECOME A DOMINANT FORCE.

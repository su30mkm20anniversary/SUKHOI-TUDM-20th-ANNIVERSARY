ABOUT THE ANNIVERSARY

Honouring 20 Years of Sukhoi Su-30MKM Service

The Sukhoi TUDM 20th Anniversary commemorates two decades of service, operational excellence and commitment to safeguarding Malaysia's sovereignty.

This milestone recognises the dedication, professionalism and contributions of the personnel who have supported the operation and maintenance of the Su-30MKM aircraft since 2007.

Join us in celebrating this significant milestone and honouring the legacy of the Sukhoi community.

MISSION & VISION

Our Mission

To commemorate 20 years of Sukhoi Su-30MKM service, recognise the dedication of personnel and celebrate the achievements that have contributed to TUDM's operational capability.

Our Vision

To preserve the legacy of the Sukhoi Su-30MKM, strengthen esprit de corps and inspire future generations to uphold excellence, professionalism and commitment to national defence.

ABOUT US

Celebrating two decades of the Sukhoi Su-30MKM in Royal Malaysian Air Force service. This milestone recognises the dedication, professionalism and commitment of all personnel involved in operating and maintaining this remarkable aircraft.


From 2007 to 2027, we honour the journey, achievements and contributions that have shaped the Sukhoi legacy in the Royal Malaysian Air Force.

MISSION & VISION

OUR MISSION

To uphold operational excellence, professionalism and commitment in ensuring the highest standards of aircraft readiness and mission effectiveness.

OUR VISION

To preserve the Sukhoi legacy through continuous improvement, teamwork and dedication towards the future of the Royal Malaysian Air Force.

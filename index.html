ABOUT THE ANNIVERSARY

Two decades of service, dedication and excellence.

20 YEARS OF SU-30MKM

The Sukhoi Su-30MKM has been an important part of the Royal Malaysian Air Force's air combat capability since its introduction in 2007.

The 20th Anniversary commemorates the journey, achievements and contributions of all personnel involved in supporting and operating this distinguished aircraft.

More than an aircraft, this anniversary represents the dedication, teamwork and professionalism of the people behind the mission.
